BOARD: Waveshare ESP32-C3 Zero
MCU: ESP32-C3FH4 (160MHz RISC-V, 400KB RAM, WiFi/BLE)
VOLTAGE: 3.3V Logic
POWER: USB-C or 5V pin

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 I2C BUS (Moved to avoid LED conflict)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SDA → GPIO 6 (Changed from 8)
SCL → GPIO 7 (Changed from 9)
VCC → 3.3V
GND → GND

Wait! GPIO 8 is the built-in LED (WS2812).
GPIO 6/7 are good candidates, but let's check availability.
Standard C3 Zero Pinout:
0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8(LED), 9(BOOT), 10, 18, 19, 20, 21

Let's use:
SDA → GPIO 6
SCL → GPIO 7
(Or standard I2C is often 8/9, but 8 is LED).

REVISED I2C PINS:
SDA → GPIO 6
SCL → GPIO 7
(These are free and ADC capable too)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 DIGITAL JOYSTICK (5-Way Switch)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
UP    → GPIO 0
DOWN  → GPIO 1
LEFT  → GPIO 2
RIGHT → GPIO 3
CLICK → GPIO 4

Wiring: Active LOW (internal pull-ups)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 CLUTCH BUTTON
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CLUTCH → GPIO 5
Wiring: Active LOW (internal pull-up)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 BATTERY MONITOR (ADC)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
VBAT → GPIO 8 (ADC) via voltage divider
Divider: 10kΩ + 10kΩ (Ratio = 2.0)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 STATUS LED (Built-in WS2812 RGB)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DIN → GPIO 10
Use Adafruit NeoPixel library!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 UART (For Slave Bridge)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TX → GPIO 21
RX → GPIO 20

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 PINOUT SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GPIO 0  → Joystick UP
GPIO 1  → Joystick DOWN
GPIO 2  → Joystick LEFT
GPIO 3  → Joystick RIGHT
GPIO 4  → Button SCALE UP
GPIO 5  → Dead-Man Clutch Button
GPIO 6  → I2C SDA (MPU6050)
GPIO 7  → I2C SCL (MPU6050)
GPIO 8  → Battery Voltage (ADC)
GPIO 9  → Button SCALE DOWN
GPIO 10 → Built-in RGB LED (WS2812)
GPIO 18 → Reserved/Free
GPIO 19 → Reserved/Free
GPIO 20 → UART RX (Bridge to Teensy)
GPIO 21 → UART TX (Bridge to Teensy)
