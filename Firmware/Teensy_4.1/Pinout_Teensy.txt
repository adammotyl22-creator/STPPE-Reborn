╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║    TEENSY 4.1 PINOUT - 6-DOF ROBOT ARM CONTROLLER (URDF)       ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

BOARD: Teensy 4.1 (600MHz ARM Cortex-M7)
VOLTAGE: 3.3V Logic (5V tolerant on most pins)
ROBOT MODEL: stppe_zlozenie_nowe (6-DOF Articulated Stepper Arm + Gripper Servo)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 STEPPER MOTORS J1–J6 (TMC2209 Drivers)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Axis 1 (Base Yaw, Z-rotation):
  STEP → Pin 3
  DIR  → Pin 2
  EN   → Pin 6
  Range: -90° .. +90°

Axis 2 (Shoulder Pitch - 20:1 Geared):
  STEP → Pin 11
  DIR  → Pin 10
  EN   → Pin 27
  Range: -65° .. +115°

Axis 3 (Elbow Pitch - 20:1 Geared):
  STEP → Pin 29
  DIR  → Pin 28
  EN   → Pin 32
  Range: -35° .. +170°

Axis 4 (Forearm Roll):
  STEP → Pin 34
  DIR  → Pin 33
  EN   → Pin 12
  Range: -90° .. +90°

Axis 5 (Wrist Pitch):
  STEP → Pin 31
  DIR  → Pin 30
  EN   → Pin 12
  Range: -90° .. +90° (Neutral 0°)

Axis 6 (Tool Flange Roll):
  STEP → Pin 23
  DIR  → Pin 22
  EN   → Pin 12
  Range: -180° .. +180° (Continuous)

TMC2209 UART Bus:
  TX/RX → Pin 17 (Serial4 TX)
  Note: Use 1kΩ pull-up to 3.3V on PDN_UART pin

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 END EFFECTOR (GRIPPER)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Gripper Servo:
  PWM  → Pin 21
  Range: 0° (Fully Closed) .. 180° (Fully Open)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 AS5600 MAGNETIC ENCODERS (Hardware I2C)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Encoder 2 (Shoulder - Wire):
  SDA → Pin 18
  SCL → Pin 19

Encoder 3 (Elbow - Wire1):
  SDA → Pin 44
  SCL → Pin 45

AS5600 Wiring (Each):
  VCC → 3.3V
  GND → GND
  DIR → GND (Clockwise)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 COMMUNICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PC Serial (USB):
  Baud: 115200

USB Host (For Master Controller):
  D+  → USB Host header D+
  D-  → USB Host header D-

ESP32 Serial (Optional):
  RX  → Pin 0 (Serial1 RX)
  TX  → Pin 1 (Serial1 TX)
  Baud: 115200

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 POWER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Teensy Power:
  VIN → 5V (USB or external)
  GND → Common Ground

Motor Power:
  TMC2209: 12-24V (separate supply)
  Gripper Servo: 5-6V (separate supply)
  
⚠️  IMPORTANT: Keep logic ground common, but isolate motor power!
